# Publishing an Already-Added Property to the Portals

**Version 1.0** | GNB-AMC User Guide

## Purpose

How to publish (make live) a property that has **already been added** to AMC onto the
property portals — Rightmove, Zoopla, OnTheMarket. Use this when the property already
exists in the system and you only need to get it live on the portals, **not** create it
from scratch.

**Applicable for:** Residential Lettings and Commercial Lettings
**Who should use this:** Agents, Property Managers, Admin Users

## Steps

1. Search for the property and open it.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Go to **Page 4 – Marketing & Portal Publishing**.
4. In the **Portal Selection** section, check that the portals you want are ticked —
   Rightmove, Zoopla, OnTheMarket. Tick any that aren't.
   *(The portals available depend on your AMC configuration.)*
5. Click **Preview** to review the details.
6. Click **Submit** to publish the property to the selected portals.

A success message confirms the property has been pushed to the selected portals.

## After publishing

- Verify the property appears under **All Properties**.
- Confirm the portal status shows **Published / Live**.

## If it doesn't publish

If the property doesn't go live after you submit, something required may be missing or
incomplete on the property (for example, the property type). Complete any missing
details and submit again. If it still won't publish, contact your Account Manager or the
helpdesk.
